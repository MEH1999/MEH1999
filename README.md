## 🛡️ About Me

I'm a **SOC Analyst** based in Maryland who defends managed client environments by day
and turns data into decisions by night. I sit at the intersection of two disciplines:

- **Security Operations** — triaging alerts from Microsoft Sentinel & Huntress EDR,
  auditing identities in Active Directory / Entra ID, and tuning detections to cut
  false positives and lower MTTR.
- **Data Analytics** — SQL & Python ETL pipelines and Tableau dashboards that turn raw
  telemetry and business data into something a decision-maker can act on.

That blend is my edge: I read logs like datasets and communicate findings clearly to
both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

🎓 B.S. Information Technology — Towson University
📜 CompTIA Security+ · Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate
